"Powerpoint Notes":"The powerpoint will consist of the slides in this order: Header, Agenda, Vocabulary, Summary + Reading, Context, Zoom, Summary, Last Bits and Reflect. The slides will only ever be in this order. The header slide will be laid out the same in every video aside from the name of the poem in light grey and a relevant image to the right to ensure consistency. The agenda slide will inform students of what is going to be discussed in the video in a bullet point list. This slide will always come directly after the header slide. The agenda will always show the same slide titles as detailed above - Vocabulary, Summary + Reading, Context, Zoom, Summary, Last Bits and Reflect.The vocabulary slide will always come directly after the agenda slide and directly before the Summary + Reading slide so that the pupils fully understand any tricky or ambiguous phrasing that is important to the poem’s meaning before reading it. Only 3-4 main phrases should be chosen to explain. This slide gives the pupils the best chance to understand the Summary + Reading part of the video. The Summary + Reading slide will have a picture of the poem/relevant imagery on the right hand side and a simplified summary of the poem and its themes on the left. The summary should be no more than 2 sentences and use concise vocabulary. The summary should be able to be read by the pupil and they should feel immediate clarity. After the Summary + Reading slide, is the context slide. It always comes directly after the Summary + Reading slide and directly before the zoom slides. It details the year the poem was written, relevant context of the time period and of the author’s life/further influences. The structure of this page may differ depending on the poet. Do not force context in a certain area if it does not require it or if it does not relate to the poem. Next is a zoom slide. It shows an important quotation from the poem. The voice over will discuss any poetic features included in the quote and their effects. This slide should be repeated 4-7 times with different powerful quotes that pupils could use in their exams. The aim is to choose the quotations that cross over as many of the exam themes and have the most impact. Next is a summary slide. It shows the quotations from the zoom slides alongside clear labelling of the feature used and the impact. This should appear in a 3 column table. It may have as many rows as zoom slides were used.The Last bits slide is an optional slide to include any extra important information that does not fit the structure given. The subtitle of Last Bits only needs to be included in the agenda slide if it is used or needed in the body of the video. Lastly, is the reflect slide.
